## 1. Fit test first
- Each pair has one knob joint. The gap is engraved on top of both halves.
- **0.20 mm** is what the puzzle uses: the pieces should drop in and lift out without force, with no visible wobble.
- If the 0.20 pair is tight and only the 0.25 pair slides freely, tell me and I'll re-cut the puzzle with 0.25 mm. If 0.15 already fits loosely, I can tighten it instead.
- Tip: tight PETG joints often loosen after you clean the elephant's foot off the bottom edge with a deburring tool or a fingernail.

## 3. Printing PETG
- Use the **Textured PEI plate**. PETG can bond too strongly to a smooth PEI plate. If you only have a smooth plate, put a thin layer of glue stick on it as a release layer.
- Keep the filament dry. Damp PETG strings, and strings between the pieces make cleanup harder.
- No supports are needed.
- The pieces are 3 mm thick at their lowest point. The codes under them are engraved 0.4 mm deep, mirrored, so they read correctly when you turn a piece over.

## 4. The puzzle
### Size
- 64 pieces on an 8 × 8 grid of about 34.5 mm.
  - The cut lines bend around buildings, so the pieces vary in shape.
  - Their footprints, knobs included, range from 31 to 56 mm.
- Height: 6.4–20.8 mm, depending on the ground and buildings. The tallest is the piece with Vezirova's minaret.
- Scale and relief are the same as the diorama:
  - Scale 1:4500, so 276 × 276 mm of model shows 1242 × 1242 m of town, north up.
  - The ground is exaggerated ×2.5.
  - Landmarks are enlarged ×2.5.

### Cuts
- **No house, landmark, bridge or sports court is cut.**
  - Every line is routed through gardens, roads or open ground.
  - All three courts sit together on piece D2.
  - Each house sits at least 0.15 mm inside its piece edge.
- **No tree, cypress or tombstone is cut.**
  - 775 trees that sat on a cut line were moved up to about 1.5 mm off it. 86 with no room to move were left out, which is 2 %.
  - Tombstone blocks next to a cut were shortened or left out.
- **Knobs:** 108 of the 112 inner edges have a classic knob.
  - Every knob neck is at least 3.2 mm wide after the gap, so knobs won't snap off.
  - In the dense centre some knobs are smaller, with a widened neck.
- **Plain edges:** four edges have no knob, because there was no room between the houses. They are between C5/C6, E6/E7, E3/E4 and E4/F4. The tray holds those pieces in place.

### Gaps
- **Between pieces:** 0.2 mm in total. Each piece edge is set back 0.1 mm.
- **Tray pocket:** 276.2 mm square, so the outer pieces have about 0.2 mm all round.

### Tray
- Outside: 300 × 300 mm. Rim: 15.9 mm tall and 12 mm wide.
- Floor: 2 mm. The rim stands 1.5 mm above the highest ground on any piece.
- The rim labels, arrows, north arrow and scale bar are raised 0.6 mm in white and red.
- GUSINJE stands 0.8 mm proud of the front face.
- Arrows on the rim point to:
  - Crkva sv. Antona / Grebaje (west).
  - Alipašini izvori / Vusanje (south).
  - ▲ Zla Kolata 2534 m (south-south-east, on the compass line from Opština to the peak).

### Plates
- **Pieces plate 1:** rows 5–8, the southern half.
- **Pieces plate 2:** rows 1–4, the northern half. Both plates keep at least 3 mm between pieces and stay clear of the prime tower.
- **Tray:** one plate. It fills x 27.5–327.5 of the bed, within the right nozzle's reach.
- **Fit test:** one plate. The halves are printed apart, and you join them by hand.

### Graveyard and sports courts
- **Graveyard (Groblje Gusinje)**
  - **Outline:** from OpenStreetMap, which matches the area you marked in white. It spans pieces A3, A4, B3, B4 and C3.
  - **Tombstones:** 242 white blocks, 0.75 mm wide, 0.6–2.4 mm long and 0.5 mm tall.
    - Each block stands for a short row of graves. The rows run north-west to south-east, so the graves inside them lie north-east to south-west, as in the graveyard.
    - They are denser where your satellite image shows more white, and they leave the paths free.
  - **Cypresses:** 310 slender cones, 0.84 mm wide and 2.3 mm tall. They stand in double rows along the graveyard paths that your drone photos show lined with cypresses.
- **Sports courts next to the Titex factory** (all on piece D2)
  - **Two red courts:** their outlines come from OpenStreetMap. They are the larger court (about 41 × 24 m) and the basketball court (about 28 × 16 m). A grey concrete strip runs around and between them.
  - **Blue tennis court:** OpenStreetMap doesn't have it. I placed it from your drone photo by matching the factory and the two red courts, to within about 2 m.
  - The courts lie flat in the ground, 0.12 mm proud, like the roads.
- **Titex factory:** its roof is now grey, as in your photo.

## 5. Assembly
- The tray floor has the guide map engraved in it: every cut line, the main streets, the river banks and each piece's code.
- Codes run from **A–H west to east** and **1–8 north to south**. A1 is the north-west corner and H8 is the south-east corner.
- Start with the edge pieces against the tray wall, then fill inwards.
- Push pieces straight down. The knobs lock sideways but the tray holds everything, so nothing needs force.

## 6. What changed from the diorama
- **Towers and minarets are thicker.** Every shaft is at least 1.5 mm wide, so they survive handling. The thin finials on top were removed.
- **Opština's portico pillars** are joined to the wall behind them.
- **Trees on a cut line** were moved slightly off it, so every tree is whole. Houses, landmarks, bridges and courts are never cut.
- **Thinner base.** The lowest ground is now 3 mm thick (it was 6.5 mm), to save filament and time.

## 7. Data and what is approximate
- The map, terrain, building heights and landmark models are the same as in the diorama. See `Gusinje_diorama_README.md` for the sources.
- **Approximate:** the 56 houses south of Čekića and near Kolenović are placed from the satellite image, because OSM doesn't have them.
- **Assumed designs:** the Sahat kula, the bell tower of St George and the Čekića minaret.
- The projects follow Bambu Studio's own file format. They were checked with my own tools, but not opened in Bambu Studio itself, because it can't run in this workspace.
