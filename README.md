# Slagalica Gusinje

The 3D-printed Gusinje jigsaw (64 pieces in a 300 × 300 mm tray), played on a phone with fingers. It runs in the browser and works offline once opened.

## How to play
- Drag a piece up out of the drawer and drop it in its place. It snaps in when it's close.
- Pinch to zoom, one or two fingers to move, both fingers up or down to tilt. Double tap zooms.
- **Lako**: the guide map and piece codes are on the tray. **Srednje**: no guide. **Teško**: no guide, and pieces are turned (tap one in the drawer to turn it).
- The bulb shows where one piece goes; the picture button shows the finished map.
- Progress is saved on the phone. When the puzzle is done you can look around the town and tap the landmark names.

## Files
- `index.html`, `css/`, `js/`: the game (three.js r184, MIT, in `js/vendor/three`).
- `data/pieces.glb`: one node per piece, named by its code (A–H west to east, 1–8 north to south), placed at its assembled position. Millimetres, Y up, -Z north. Vertex colours; alpha 128 marks water.
- `data/tray.glb`: the tray. `data/puzzle.json`: tray size, piece outlines and positions, landmarks.
- `sw.js`: offline cache, generated by `tools/build_sw.py`.

## Rebuilding the data
- `tools/standin.py <dir>`: temporary stand-in pieces in the same format.
- `tools/pack.sh <dir>`: compresses the GLBs with gltfpack (meshoptimizer) into `data/` and refreshes `sw.js`.
- `tests/play.cjs <url>`: plays the game in a phone-size Chromium with real touch events.

Map data © OpenStreetMap contributors (ODbL). Terrain: Copernicus DEM.
